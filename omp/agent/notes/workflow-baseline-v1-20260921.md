# Workflow baseline v1 — active policy

This is the maintained English policy source for the accepted 2026-09-20 workflow baseline. Its decision provenance is `<local>/OMP-工作流决策基线-v1.md`; that Chinese file remains the approved original. `workflow-decisions.md` is retained historical rationale, not an active competing rule. Apply this note to new work only. Existing frozen contracts, role/model choices, fallback chains, controller gates, and incomplete work keep their recorded terms.

## Scope, contract, and planning

At scope start, Sol records whether the work is narrow maintenance or a complete product. Narrow maintenance uses the relevant behavior and risk checks; a complete product must not be relabelled as maintenance to evade accepted final roles. A complete product has one maintained contract containing the accepted outcomes, architecture and interface boundaries, ownership, delivery/install location, observable acceptance, and a task plan. These are required contents, not four mandatory documents. A decision has one authoritative location; dispatches cite its path and version.

Purple PLAN reviews a complete contract once before dependent implementation. Repeat only for a material change to scope, architecture, cross-module interface, or acceptance, and review the change and its impact rather than reopening the whole plan. PLAN findings and disposition remain recorded; Purple is read-only. Matt is an optional method library, not an agent, controller, or approval phase.

Plan work by an observable business capability, then split it into stable-interface, single-owner execution packages. A task package uses Goal, Decisions, Ownership, Checks, Stop, and Handoff, including the contract version, dependency maturity, exact paths/resources, focused checks, and usable handoff. Reuse existing foundations; assign and prove only a missing shared foundation that dependent work actually needs. A high-impact unknown gets the smallest real end-to-end proof before fan-out. An initial author fixes its own implementation and focused-check failures, including corrections prompted by development-time visual assistance.

## Bounded execution and integration

Keep at most four active writing authors. When a slot opens, dispatch the next substantive package whose declared inputs are mature and whose paths and shared invariants do not conflict; do not wait for unrelated work or manufacture tasks to fill a slot. Candidate integration remains serialized and runs the agreed automated candidate check. Do not dispatch mandatory intermediate red/blue reviewers merely because an author or candidate finished; author checks, real integration, and automated candidate checks remain required.

Any failure or uncertain execution pauses new dispatch. Preserve evidence and collect the status of already-running work. Sol may explicitly resume an inspected, proven-independent package; there is no automatic retry, cleanup, repair continuation, or resume. A completed process is not a handoff: the author must settle writes, focused checks, and evidence.

For a new controlled `integration-v1` run, `run_begin` freezes `executionPolicy: "integration-v1"`, `requiredGates: ["integration_check"]`, the argv `checkCommand`, and `diagnosticEvidence: { schema: "omp-check-diagnostic/v1", root }`. For a dependency-bearing merged candidate, the plan also freezes an argv `candidateSetupCommand` (for example `bun install --frozen-lockfile`); it runs in the candidate's actual cwd immediately before the frozen check, records an independent setup receipt, and has no automatic retry. A dependency-free candidate records its explicit skipped setup receipt; a missing required command fails closed. The root explicitly authorizes that existing evidence directory outside the reviewed source. The root alone invokes `run_check` with the run identifiers and an idempotency key; it runs that frozen command in the canonical root and records source identity before and after execution. `integration-v1` is rejected for high-risk work. A run that omits the execution policy retains legacy gates; already-frozen runs retain their recorded gate and evidence contracts. This describes the policy interface; deployment availability must be verified separately.

`run_check` keeps byte counts, SHA-256 digests, stream status, and truncation information in ordinary receipts. New integration runs also require a bounded, sanitized diagnostic artifact in the authorized directory, with its actual path, integrity and availability recorded; raw stdout or stderr must not be persisted. Missing required evidence cannot count as complete, and a diagnostic failure must preserve the original process result. Already-frozen runs without this evidence policy retain their historical digest-only behavior. The existing 30-minute execution timeout remains the bound, and output volume alone does not create a new execution cap.

## Final review, repair, and evidence

Freeze one final candidate: all required writing has stopped and source, build, owned runtime, and known ignore rules/startup documentation are identified. Read source SHA/fingerprints programmatically and retain artifacts outside source. The named runtime owner keeps the preview/version/build alive until its reviewers and other consumers settle. Run the full final red and blue reviews in parallel and, for a user-facing product, the applicable independent visual and real-user-experience reviews in parallel where resources can be isolated. Do not cancel accepted final roles after another review fails. Purple DELIVERY collects and reconciles requirements, contract changes, evidence, actual artifacts, and documented launch/install checks even when a technical review has already failed; it cannot approve a candidate with a defect or coverage gap.

After all executable review work is collected, Sol deduplicates findings by root cause. Confirmed final-review defects transfer to Terra repair packages after earlier writers settle. Truly independent packages may run in parallel within the same ownership and four-author limits. Terra does not accept its own repair. Freeze the repaired candidate and have the relevant independent roles recheck the findings, affected path, and necessary regression. New findings form the next batch.

Receipts bind to the current source, workspace, environment, and reviewer task. Canonical reviewer receipts use top-level `status`, nonblank `summary`, nonempty `evidence`, and `findings`; a valid top-level `status: "PASS"` output remains structured, but the runtime persists an `INCONCLUSIVE` receipt when `deliveryReady` is false or a prerequisite is unsettled. Dispatch final-ready work only after those current-source prerequisites settle, while continuing to collect executable independent failures. After a fingerprint change, every mandatory final role issues a new current-source incremental receipt for affected scope and attests unchanged valid coverage; unchanged tests need not be rerun mechanically. An older PASS is evidence only, never the current receipt. Preserve original failures and distinguish product defects, unavailable environments, and missing coverage.

## Visual help, diagrams, and delivery

Visual help during implementation follows actual capability, not an agent name. A nonvisual frontend author gets scoped visual feedback when a shared UI foundation first forms and later when the layout or observation requires it; the author owns resulting corrections. Final visual acceptance is independent and cannot be replaced by that feedback. New v4 acceptance requires an explicit root assignment of final purpose for every mandatory final role, bound by runtime dispatch to the concrete task, source, workspace and review window. Missing or unknown purpose never grants final eligibility; development feedback remains separate. Existing v2/v3 checkpoints retain their recorded semantics, and unknown checkpoint versions fail closed. Do not resume v4 checkpoints or diagnostic-policy runs with older code that does not enforce their contracts; preserve their state and evidence until compatible handling is available.

Text is the contract authority. For a multi-module product or multi-agent work, keep architecture/data-flow and task dependency/concurrency views; simple single-owner work can use one compact view or no new diagram when the existing representation remains sufficient. Purple PLAN does not wait for rendering. An assigned existing author may generate the necessary Archify JSON and HTML in parallel with implementation; no separate permanent or mandatory diagram author exists. Validate required diagrams against implementation before final freeze, but do not create a separate product review loop for them.

Completion requires current acceptance, agreed artifacts at their agreed destinations, and executable documented launch/install checks where promised. Optional archive transport and cleanup occur only after delivery and are not default completion gates. They never authorize deletion of evidence, worktrees, services, user data, or remote publication.
